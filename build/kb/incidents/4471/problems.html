What engineering did

  • parser — fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Reports from day-first tenants through app, email and DD/MM/YYYY forms should stop.
  • hint — won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, so its MM/DD/YYYY hint goes with it. Tenants still on booking-v3 need to move off it.
  • one-day — nothing yet. The booking lands one day early: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May” (R-41202), “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday” (R-41344), “The invite arrived before I made the booking” (R-41236). A day/month flip cannot do that, so the parser fix does not close it. The latest came in on 4 October (R-41620).

How each report was placed

  1. One report per id. The export repeats some reports across its pages; each id counts once.
  2. hint — a day-first tenant on booking-v3, reporting through the form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first as told, and the form reads it the tenant’s way.
  3. parser — every other report from a day-first tenant. This is the desk’s own count (causes.ts), the same as the table on #4471.
  4. one-day — the reports the desk itself lists in desk-notes.json as a one-day move. The desk counts them under parser, where they are filed; they are also a problem of their own.

The intake tag (meta.intakeTag) is not used. It is guessed from the reporter’s browser and often disagrees with the tenant’s locale and form (see the tag column on #4471).

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so all three landed in #4471 and inherited its “known” status, and then its close.
