  4. parser — every other report from a day-first tenant: app, email, and forms that print DD/MM/YYYY. This is the bug #4471 is named for.

The intake tag (meta.intakeTag) is not used. It is guessed from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant’s locale and form often (see the tag column on #4471).
